# Reconstitution Ledger 001

## WHAT SURVIVED
Alpha 1's small deterministic reducer survives as an experimental specimen because it encodes a useful boundary cheaply: an actor cannot label its own evidence origin and pass the explicit self-certification check.

## WHAT DIED
The assumption that the 94→36 staging topology is the target architecture. It is archaeological organization only.

## WHAT MUTATED
Recovery is now evolutionary selection. Repositories supply candidate mechanisms; mechanisms compete on observed capability per irreversible complexity.

## WHAT EXECUTED
The existing genesis test is historical/current repository evidence but has not been re-run by this connector. Its source was inspected.

## WHAT FAILED
Inspection exposed a semantic verification hole: `evidence.origin` is caller-controlled. Changing the string from the actor name to another label causes the reducer to accept an OBSERVED event without dereferencing evidence, recomputing a hash, or authenticating a verifier.

## WHAT NEW CAPABILITY EMERGED
A machine-readable capability graph now treats the existing Alpha reducer and recovered NGL mechanisms as competitors rather than sacred project boundaries. A regression specimen preserves the first discovered weakness.

## EVIDENCE

- `reconstitution/capability-graph.v1.json`: explicit statuses and selection pressure.
