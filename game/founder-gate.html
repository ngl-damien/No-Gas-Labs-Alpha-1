A FOUNDING OBSERVANCE

Something real.
Held to a real standard.

This inaugural Founders Day commemorates the making of No_Gas_Labs: the ideas given form, the failures kept in view, and the patient work of making a claim answerable to evidence.

It honours a beginning. It leaves room for what has yet to be proved.

Read the inaugural invocation
Here, in Carter Lake, Iowa, we mark a beginning: the inaugural Founders Day of No_Gas_Labs™.

One person began with an idea. Through persistent work with AI, that idea has taken form as a world, a workshop, a record of evidence, and an unfinished responsibility.

We honour the work that holds up under examination. We keep the failures that taught us how to improve it. We leave the next person a record they can inspect and carry forward.

AI is labor. Claims require evidence. The Neophyte Founder™ retains institutional authority.

May this institution become more useful, more capable, and more honest with each cycle of its work.

Let this first Founders Day leave a record worth returning to.
