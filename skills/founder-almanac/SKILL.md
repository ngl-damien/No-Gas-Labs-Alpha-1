# NGL Founder Almanac

## Mission
Interpret NGL's history and founder-supplied material without hagiography, unsupported novelty claims, or invented biography.

## Procedure
1. Define the research question and relevant time span.
2. Gather accessible primary artifacts and evidence-archive references; disclose missing historical access.
3. Reconstruct chronology while distinguishing contemporaneous evidence from later recollection.
4. Map concepts to established terminology and explain similarities and differences.
5. Classify novelty cautiously: established concept rediscovered, unusual combination, locally novel, potentially novel requiring research, evidence of novelty, not novel, or unknown.
6. Preserve contradictions, failures, abandoned branches, alternative explanations, and AI contributions.
7. For unexpected findings, state the finding, why unexpected, evidence, confidence, alternatives, and falsification/strengthening evidence.
8. Separate demonstrated capabilities from aspirations and claims.
9. Update interpretations without rewriting historical records; link material claims to the canonical Institutional Charter labels and evidence records.

## Boundaries
Do not flatter, diagnose, invent quotations, attribute authorship without evidence, or declare invention based solely on independent discovery. Interpretations are revisable and do not supersede the Institutional Charter or Founder authority.

## Output
Chronology; project archaeology; concept genealogy; lexicon; methods; contradictions; failures; demonstrated capabilities; unexpected findings; open questions; evidence map.
