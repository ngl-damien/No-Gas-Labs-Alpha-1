### ARTICLE I — PURPOSE

No_Gas_Labs™ shall undertake a comprehensive adversarial qualification of Alpha 1 before unrestricted public exposure.

The purpose is not to demonstrate that Alpha 1 can function under favorable conditions. It is to establish whether the institution's architecture, engineering, evidence, governance, continuity, and claims can withstand examination by competent specialists operating under realistic adversarial assumptions.

The primary threat under investigation is **catastrophic institutional discrediting through demonstrable technical, methodological, or structural inadequacy**.

No_Gas_Labs™ shall not confuse successful demonstrations, passing tests, persuasive explanations, or improvements over inferior baselines with professional adequacy.

**The institution shall be capable of discovering its own inadequacies before demanding that the public trust its capabilities.**

### ARTICLE II — SCOPE OF EXAMINATION

The commission shall examine Alpha 1 across at least seven independently evaluated domains:

1. **Software engineering:** Architecture, correctness, integration, maintainability, dependency integrity, reproducibility, and failure isolation.
2. **Cybersecurity:** Founder authentication, authorization boundaries, privilege separation, hostile inputs, secrets, supply-chain integrity, persistence, recovery, and resistance to forged evidence.
3. **AI and autonomous systems:** Actual versus claimed autonomy, task reliability, model dependence, cross-provider continuity, agent coordination, performance measurement, and the validity of self-improvement claims.
4. **Scientific and experimental methodology:** Hypothesis formation, falsifiability, controls, measurement validity, confounding variables, replication, negative results, and independent verification.
5. **Institutional governance:** Founder authority, delegated capabilities, decision legitimacy, conflict resolution, institutional memory, accountability, and continuity independent of a particular AI provider.
6. **Economics and external utility:** The difference between technical output and customer value, operational sustainability, distribution, commercial viability, and verifiable financial outcomes.
7. **Human participation and world systems:** The RPG/TownSquare interface, participant roles, Witness capabilities, access boundaries, persistence, interaction safety, and the correspondence between represented accomplishments and actual evidence.

Additional disciplines shall be introduced when findings justify their relevance. No domain may certify another domain merely by association.

### ARTICLE III — ADVERSARIAL METHOD

For every domain, the executing system shall:

1. Inventory the actual relevant implementations and supporting evidence.
2. Identify the strongest defensible capability claims.
3. Identify assumptions on which those claims depend.
4. Construct realistic specialist-grade challenges capable of falsifying those claims.
5. Execute reproducible negative tests where authorized and safe.
6. Preserve failures, contradictions, measurements, and environmental constraints.
7. Distinguish confirmed vulnerabilities from hypotheses, untested concerns, and unavailable evidence.
8. Produce competing repairs rather than automatically adopting the first proposed solution.
9. Test repairs against both the original failure and existing regression requirements.
10. Reassess whether the corrected system meets an appropriate professional adequacy threshold.

Internal AI criticism shall be identified as internal criticism. Simulated specialists shall not be represented as independent human specialists.

**No system may certify its own professional adequacy solely through tests or standards that it controls.**

### ARTICLE IV — THE THREE-GATE QUALIFICATION STANDARD

Every material institutional capability shall face three independent assessments.

**Gate A — Evolutionary Fitness:** Does the proposed implementation measurably outperform or extend the incumbent under comparable, documented conditions without unacceptable regressions?

**Gate B — Professional Adequacy:** Does the resulting capability satisfy explicit, externally defensible engineering, security, scientific, and operational requirements appropriate to its intended use?

**Gate C — Adversarial Survivability:** When subjected to informed criticism or failure, can the institution detect the problem, preserve evidence, contain consequences, construct and evaluate repairs, and recover without falsifying its history or compromising Founder authority?

Passing Gate A does not imply passing Gate B. Passing Gates A and B does not imply passing Gate C.

**A capability is not qualified until all applicable gates have supporting evidence.**

### ARTICLE V — CATASTROPHIC FAILURE INVESTIGATION

The commission shall prioritize failures capable of invalidating multiple institutional capabilities simultaneously. These include:

- Unauthorized transfer or impersonation of Founder authority.
- Destruction, corruption, or unverifiable alteration of institutional memory.
- False attribution of independent verification, execution, or external participation.
- Candidate-controlled manipulation of evaluators or promotion standards.
- Critical security failures affecting evidence, identities, or decision-making.
- Inability to restore operations from preserved source and institutional state.
- Claims of autonomous institutional improvement unsupported by reproducible transfer experiments.
- Architectural dependencies whose failure disables otherwise independent institutional functions.

Each identified failure mode shall be assessed for impact, exploitability or triggering conditions, detectability, recoverability, and available mitigation.

The commission shall never invent a catastrophic vulnerability merely to justify additional development.

### ARTICLE VI — CONTINUITY AND SELF-IMPROVEMENT

Adversarial qualification must improve the institution rather than produce an isolated criticism report.

At least one material finding shall be processed through a complete engineering cycle:

**Discovery → Reproduction → Competing repairs → Independent evaluation → Authorized disposition → Regression verification → Preservation → Transfer test.**

The resulting improvement must be evaluated on a subsequent, distinct task.

The commission shall measure whether Alpha 1 becomes more capable of detecting, preventing, or recovering from comparable defects while reducing unnecessary Founder intervention.

The Founder shall retain direction, judgment, purpose, and protected authorization.

Routine coordination, research, testing, documentation, and recovery shall be delegated wherever technically supportable and authorized.

### ARTICLE VII — NON-DESTRUCTIVE EXECUTION

The commission is authorized to conduct repository inspection, local testing, analysis, reproducible experiments, and reversible development work within available permissions.

It does not authorize destructive penetration of third-party systems, disclosure of credentials, irreversible production changes, spending, public deployment, or modification of Founder authority.

Experiments that might jeopardize canonical source, private records, or operational environments shall use appropriately isolated copies and verified recovery procedures.

Apparent obstacles shall be investigated before being classified as confirmed blockers. A blocker must have demonstrable impact and a record of applicable authorized alternatives attempted.

### ARTICLE VIII — EVIDENCE AND QUALIFICATION RECORDS

Every substantial finding shall have:

- A unique identifier and affected capability.
- The precise claim or assumption tested.
- The relevant specialist discipline.
- Evidence references and reproducible test instructions.
- An epistemic classification: CONFIRMED, REPRODUCED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN, or REFUTED.
- An impact and risk assessment.
- Repair alternatives and their evaluation results.
- Remaining limitations and rollback provisions.
- A disposition and the authority responsible for that disposition.

All qualification records shall remain discoverable through the institution's evidence and continuity systems. Failures shall not be erased by subsequent successful repairs.

### ARTICLE IX — PUBLIC EXPOSURE POLICY

Unrestricted public launch of Alpha 1 shall not be recommended solely because the system is functional, compelling, visually impressive, or internally well tested.

Before launch, the commission must produce a defensible assessment of the risks associated with the intended exposure.

Public-facing capabilities may be considered separately according to their demonstrated security, reliability, and isolation. Qualification of one interface shall not silently authorize exposure of the entire institution.

Where qualified independent human specialist review is unavailable, the report shall explicitly identify that limitation.

The objective is not immunity from criticism. It is to prevent foreseeable, foundational deficiencies from being concealed beneath ambitious claims.

### ARTICLE X — COMPLETION CRITERION

This commission shall not be considered complete upon producing an audit report, identifying vulnerabilities, passing a test suite, or presenting a proposed architectural improvement.

A successful qualification cycle must demonstrate:

1. A preserved and inspectable baseline of Alpha 1.
2. Executed adversarial tests spanning all seven domains, with meaningful tests and limitations documented for each.
3. Reproducible evidence for confirmed findings, including appropriately prioritized severe defects.
4. At least one consequential repair or hardening improvement selected through comparative evaluation.
5. Successful regression verification and recovery where applicable.
6. A distinct follow-on task demonstrating whether the improvement transfers.
7. Continued integrity of Founder authority and institutional evidence.
8. A specialist-readable qualification dossier distinguishing supported capabilities from outstanding deficiencies.
9. A Founder Gate decision presenting the evidence, residual risks, and available authorization choices.
10. A defensible recommendation concerning the readiness of specific capabilities for controlled external examination.

Completion of this internal qualification does **not** constitute independent specialist certification, public-launch authorization, or proof of sustained autonomous growth.

### ARTICLE XI — FOUNDER COMPLETION TOKEN

Upon genuine completion, the executing system shall present a verified, authenticated Founder Gate containing a consequential qualification decision.

The notification shall identify the actual operational URL, decision identifier, review deadline, and supporting evidence.

The Founder must be able to inspect the qualification dossier and determine whether to:

**AUTHORIZE CONTROLLED EXTERNAL SPECIALIST REVIEW; REQUIRE FURTHER HARDENING; or REJECT THE PRESENT QUALIFICATION.**

No default approval shall be inferred from silence, elapsed time, or AI recommendation.

A simulated gate, local demonstration, unverified URL, or statement that work is complete shall not satisfy this requirement.

No_Gas_Labs™ is not commissioned to appear sophisticated. It is commissioned to become capable.

Its objective is not to persuade specialists that it cannot fail, but to demonstrate that its capabilities, methods, and evidence remain defensible under serious scrutiny—and that discovering a weakness makes the institution better rather than rendering its work meaningless.

**Preserve the ambition. Attack the assumptions. Strengthen the institution. Do not collapse its distinct capabilities. Do not promote confidence beyond evidence.**
