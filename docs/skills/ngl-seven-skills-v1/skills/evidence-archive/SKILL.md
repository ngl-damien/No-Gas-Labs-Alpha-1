# NGL Evidence Archive

## Mission
Make institutional claims traceable to inspectable sources and preserve the distinction between evidence and interpretation.

## Procedure
1. Identify the claim, event, decision, artifact, or execution to be recorded.
2. Capture source identity, location, timestamp or date, author/actor if known, scope, version/hash when available, and access limitations.
3. Assign an epistemic label: USER_STATED, ARTIFACT_OBSERVED, IMPLEMENTED, EXECUTION_OBSERVED, INFERRED, CONTRADICTED, or UNKNOWN.
4. Preserve original material or a stable reference; do not silently rewrite it.
5. Link derived summaries to their supporting sources and mark omissions.
6. Record corrections as new versions or amendments where possible.
7. Distinguish historical evidence from current verification.
8. Report missing, inaccessible, conflicting, or stale evidence.

## Boundaries
Do not fabricate timestamps, hashes, receipts, source access, or persistent storage. Do not turn an interpretation into a historical fact.
## Output
Evidence record; provenance; claim-to-source mapping; confidence and limitations; conflicts; preservation status; follow-up needed.
