# NGL-257 — ENDORSEAM™

Research record · 2026-10-15 · No_Gas_Labs™

ENDORSEAM is an offline documentary interval examiner for certificate-of-insurance and endorsement evidence. It compares a project work window against operator-entered policy declarations, certificate assertions, and project-scoped endorsement copies. Its output separates documented-in-input, asserted-only, and unsupported intervals, with dated calculation witnesses.

**Status:** locally implemented and internally tested; this repository record is not the executable release, a buyer validation, or an insurance coverage certification.

**Research market evidence:** Certificial advertises requestor pricing from $99/month and free tracking for up to five suppliers (https://www.certificial.com/pricing); StrikeDocs offers a free AI COI checker (https://strikedocs.com/); the NY Department of Financial Services states that a certificate does not confer additional policy rights (https://www.dfs.ny.gov/insurance/ogco2008/rg080513.htm).

**Limitations:** no document authentication, cancellation verification, insurer integration, PDF extraction, legal interpretation, or proof of actual coverage. All demonstrations are synthetic.

**Proposed price:** $49 one-time, not market-validated. No external buyer trial, purchase, or revenue evidenced.

**Institutional rule:** AI is labor; an AI assertion is never sufficient evidence.
