# NGL-273 — REGIMEFORK™

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: local product package completed; market gate HOLD; independent buyer validation absent.

REGIMEFORK is an offline, exact finite-domain policy revision examiner. It partitions inclusive integer ranges at every rule boundary, cross-products enumerated fields, identifies uncovered and conflicting states, reports shadowed rules, and calculates before/after first-match decision changes with representative test witnesses.

Fictional demonstration: 2,000 modeled states; a two-state gap (order_value 500 across two customer types); 200 modeled states with conflicting overlapping rules; a shadowed exception; and a missing source-reference label. These are not observed customers or financial outcomes.

Local testing: 500 Python/JavaScript differential dossiers (zero disagreements); 500 independent brute-force oracles; 400 deliberately narrow synthetic regressions detected by exact partition and missed by a four-point baseline; browser checks at 1440/390/320 px. The benchmark does not estimate real-world error prevalence.

Market context: GoRules has a free tier and paid plans, and ToolHub provides a free local decision-table integrity validator. Proposed one-time price $49 is a hypothesis, not a validated offer. No independent trials, traffic, purchase requests, sales or revenue are evidenced.
