# NGL-142 — COVERAGEFRONTIER (research record)

No_Gas_Labs™ Digital Product Experiment · 2026-10-10

**Question:** Does a vendor insurance evidence packet support the obligations entered by an operator, rather than merely repeat a certificate's claims?

**Artifact:** An offline HTML packet examiner and reproducible ZIP were created and archived in the private NGL Catalog. This branch records research only; it does **not** publish the commercial executable.

**Mechanism:** Structured evidence-type firewall distinguishing certificates, policy declarations and endorsements; recorded limit/date/beneficiary/scope checks; optional SHA-256 source-file binding; document-request frontier; deterministic receipts.

**Demonstrations:** Synthetic adversarial packet: HOLD (4 blocking contradictions, 4 reviews); clean control: BOUNDED; incomplete and blanket controls: REVIEW. BOUNDED does not authenticate insurance coverage.

**Quality evidence:** 20 named Node tests passed; 420 independent Python scenarios with 2,948 assertions; 52 desktop/mobile browser assertions; 51-file deterministic ZIP. Mobile direct-file navigation remains unverified.

**Market evidence:** Texas Department of Insurance ACORD 25 example and FAQ establish that certificates do not themselves confer coverage rights. Competitors include TrustLayer (free up to 50 vendors), COI Tracker (free up to 10), and paid endorsement-checking tools. These are problem and competition observations, not purchase evidence.

**Proposed price:** $39 one-time. **Market gate:** conditional pass. **Quality gate:** pass within defined scope. **Commercial evidence gate:** HOLD.

**Not evidenced:** public executable release, buyer outreach, independent use, purchase requests, sales, or revenue.

This instrument does not verify insurers, determine actual coverage, or give legal or insurance advice.
