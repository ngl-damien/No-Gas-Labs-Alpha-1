## Required control flow

1. **Reconnaissance:** T.R.U. assembles a timestamped, provenance-linked history of a real NGL capability and its prior versions. It separates OBSERVED, REPORTED, INFERRED, SIMULATED and FICTIONAL assertions. No epoch crossover upgrades evidence status.
2. **Generation:** AOTA² nominates at least one real candidate alongside a genuine incumbent, specifying the same workload, environment, expected outputs, measurable costs, threat assumptions and rollback identifier.
3. **Adversarial evaluation:** An evaluator outside candidate-controlled code runs both, tests negative paths, captures commands, versions, outputs and failures, and produces independently checkable execution receipts where supported.
4. **Evidence retention:** RAE and EvidenceOS (or evidence-backed existing equivalents) preserve hashes, signatures if available, provenance, missing evidence, contradictions, evaluator version and an append-only decision history. A label never substitutes for attestation.
5. **Gate creation:** A server-side operation creates an authentic pending decision from that evaluation, including incumbent/candidate identifiers, typed receipts, safety caveats, potential consequences, rollback plan, deadline, and authority scope.
6. **Founder decision:** A founder-authenticated HTTPS interface presents the record with APPROVE, REJECT and DEFER where policy permits. The server enforces expiration, idempotency, authorization and anti-replay constraints. Approval is not automatic promotion: actual protected effects require the applicable authorization and safety checks.
7. **Disposition and recovery:** Record the authorized result; if action occurs, preserve the before/after state and exercise rollback or verified recovery. T.R.U. adds the disposition to history for the next investigation.

Every edge must have a recorded producer, consumer, schema, validation rule, and failure response. Create a machine-readable sample of the actual trace, not just a diagram.

## Execution rules: preserve breadth, control sprawl

**Consolidate duplication without collapsing capabilities.** Reuse implementation where semantics match; preserve separate interfaces, schemas, test cases, ownership, and audit trails for unlike duties. Do not rebrand a local integrity check as independent attestation or a fiction case as a production event.

Build in executable dependency order: inventory and tests -> baseline/trace -> evaluation receipts -> temporal lineage -> server-side decision -> authenticated gate -> deployed end-to-end verification. Parallelize nondependent work, but do not treat the phase sequence as separate finish lines.

Integrate with the existing NGL runtime, preferring a native Node interface or a justified compatibility adapter over an unmaintained parallel Python system. Every proposed change needs a disposition: REUSE, ADAPT, REPLACE (with migration and rollback), RETAIN SEPARATELY (with reason), or REJECT.

### Adversarial requirements

Attack fabricated or relabeled test/runtime/external files; hard links, duplicate bytes, raceable symlinks, mutable inputs, bad manifests, Unicode or traversal paths, stale/replayed receipts, and CI-green-without-execution. Prove a candidate cannot modify evaluator policy or grant its own promotion. Test invalid signatures or unverifiable witness claims honestly; do not invent a trusted signer. Exercise authenticated versus unauthenticated access, expired decisions, CSRF or replay, duplicate submission, restart persistence, rollback and evidence disclosure controls at the actual endpoint.

### Investigate obstacles; never invent them

An apparent missing credential, domain, hosting facility, or permission is a **dependency hypothesis**, not a blocker. Inspect actual infrastructure, verify access and permissions, attempt applicable authorized and zero-cost alternatives, preserve failed commands/results, and continue all independent safe work. Escalate the narrowly scoped founder action if necessary. A **CONFIRMED BLOCKER** requires evidence of real impact and exhausted applicable authorized alternatives; it is still **NOT COMPLETE**. Do not bypass or impersonate Founder authentication, spend money, change protected authority, publish publicly where approval is needed, or take irreversible actions without permission.

A time-sensitive deadline must be a genuine operational review interval grounded in current policy or explicitly authorized by the Founder, never an invented emergency.
