# Alpha 1 — Institutional Self-Amplification Thesis

**Commissioning premise:** One person begins without technical expertise, money, or staff, and uses AI collaboration to establish an institution that becomes increasingly capable of doing consequential work and improving the machinery that performs it. AI provides increasingly substantial execution; the Neophyte Founder™ provides direction, priorities, judgment, purpose, and protected authorization. No requirement for the Founder to supervise every microscopic step.

**Status:** research and engineering hypothesis requiring direct tests, not a demonstrated institutional achievement. This charter extends—does not replace—the [reconciled AOTA² mission](CODEX-MISSION.md) and its [interface contract](RECONCILIATION-CONTRACT.md).

## 1. Organism, not a single approval gate

Preserve distinguishable, cooperating functions:

1. **Research and question formation.** Turn observed problems, contradictions, and relevant external findings into explicit hypotheses and experimentally decidable questions. Record source, uncertainty, alternative explanations and what finding would change the build.
2. **Creation and competitive engineering (AOTA²).** Produce candidate implementations against a frozen incumbent, workload and evaluation policy. Measure functional correctness, security, reliability, time and resources. Archive rejected candidates and failure modes.
3. **Compounding improvement.** Improve a *reusable part of the machinery* (test harness, agent handoff, verifier, workflow, recovery, code generator, research apparatus), not merely the task's final artifact. Independently demonstrate that the improvement carries forward to a second real task.
4. **Institutional continuity.** Persist discoverable project state, responsible roles, evidence, active/blocked tasks, authority boundaries, unfinished work, recovery instructions and next executable actions independent of a single conversation, model, device or environment.
5. **World and Witness participation.** Treat the mobile-first, Endless Online-inspired NGL RPG/TownSquare as a real interface to projects, discoveries, disagreements and accomplishments. Maintain the distinction between viewer, character-bearing resident, and authenticated participant roles; do not fabricate active Witnesses. Use existing world implementations when available; never let an empty mockup represent a living community.
6. **Useful external output.** Publish or deliver a working, independently inspectable software tool, report, research artifact, creative work or other resource for an actual user. A repository existence claim alone does not establish utility, adoption, publication, or revenue.
7. **Governance and provenance.** RAE/EvidenceOS preserve receipts and limitations; T.R.U. reconstructs temporal lineage; MPPT coordinates work; the Founder Gate presents consequential decisions with explicit authority. Access or approval never substitutes for truth.

These are independently assessable capacities. They may share one deployable runtime, but MUST NOT be described as interchangeable.

## 2. The two-task compounding experiment

**Task A — consequential work.** Select a real, bounded NGL problem based on verified existing work or authentic outside need, not a toy case. Record its definition, deliverable, acceptance tests and the starting machinery version `M0`. Complete the task and generate a useful artifact `A`, tested against its own requirements. Capture the effort/cost/failure profile with reproducible instruments where practical.

**Machinery improvement — M0 → M1.** Inspect the task A work trace to isolate a repeatable friction point or failure mode. Propose at least two possible responses (which may include retaining M0) and run an incumbent-versus-candidate evaluation on an unchanged test workload. Protect evaluator and policy from candidate edits. Promote M1 only with supported evidence and appropriate Founder authorization; otherwise retain M0 and document the result. The resulting change MUST be a reusable process, engine, verifier, agent handoff, integration or workflow mechanism rather than an artifact-specific patch.

**Task B — transfer test.** Select a distinct, real follow-on NGL task not used to tune M1. Freeze its input and acceptance criteria before measuring M1. Run or compare M0 and M1 under comparable conditions on Task B (parallel isolated replay is preferred if safe; otherwise justified paired trials). Record task B deliverable `B`, quality/security regressions, actual execution evidence, any savings or capability expansion, and counterexamples. An improvement that only makes Task A look better is not demonstrated compounding.

**Evidence of improvement.** Predeclare at least one measurable success criterion appropriate to the mechanism: reduced verified defects or recovery failures, higher task completion, greater accepted coverage, lower human interventions at equal quality, lower cost/runtime at equal quality, or a previously impossible verified operation. A faster but less correct implementation is not an improvement. Report uncertainty and inconclusive outcomes honestly. Do not invent percentages, cost or control measurements.

**One-founder burden.** Track actual human interventions by category: direction, strategic judgment, consent/authorization, credential entry, correction, clerical coordination, routine retries. The demonstration must show at least one category of routine work measurably delegated or eliminated while retaining Founder control over consequential decisions; otherwise it has not tested the user-burden hypothesis.

## 3. Continuity experiment

After Task A and improvement, perform a clean-context handoff to a separate execution session/process using only durable in-repo or otherwise preserved state, without relying on a prior chat transcript. Recipient must recover: institutional charter and authority, Task A receipts, M0/M1 lineage, open failures and contradictions, Task B's acceptance criteria, next action, rollback path and active Founder decision. Document what was recovered and what failed. Avoid using undocumented assistant memory as a hidden dependency.

A crash or restart recovery test must establish persistent evidence and decision data. A diagram does not pass either test.

## 4. Witness / RPG interface acceptance

The institution's world is not a decorative skin: it must expose grounded world objects for at least one real project, one evidence-linked accomplishment, one live or archived contradiction/dispute, and one consequential decision/status. Model viewer versus resident versus Founder permissions explicitly. If publicly accessible real Witness accounts are not yet authorized or technically feasible, implement and test the permission and event interfaces against synthetic identities and mark **WITNESS PARTICIPATION UNVERIFIED**. Never claim the RPG or community is live unless an actual external user can reach it and its roles have been checked.

This is an institutional capability track; do not treat its absence as evidence that the two-task engine succeeded or falsely claim the full RPG has been shipped.

## 5. Deliverable and public usefulness

At least one output from Tasks A/B must be usable outside Codex's private context with instructions, validation, a real entry point and recoverable version reference. Explicitly distinguish *created*, *tested locally*, *deployed*, *accessible to an outsider*, *used by an outsider*, and *paid for*. No claim of adoption or sales without observed external evidence.

## 6. Milestones without substituting one for another

- **M1 Evidence:** existing implementation inventory + protected reproducible baseline.
- **M2 Useful work:** Task A finished with accessible artifact, tests, and provenance.
- **M3 Mechanism:** independent M0-vs-M1 comparison, authorized disposition and rollback.
- **M4 Transfer:** Task B completed using M1 and fairly compared with M0; predeclared improvement met without regressions.
- **M5 Continuity:** fresh-context recovery and restart persistence verified.
- **M6 Embodied interface:** RPG/TownSquare project, evidence, dispute and decision representations with permission tests; real Witness participation only if independently observed.
- **M7 Founder decision:** live verified HTTPS Founder Gate with genuine time-sensitive decision, authenticated Founder controls and preserved audit history.

**Alpha 1 strong success condition:** M2 + M3 + M4 + M5 + M7, plus an honest, tested M6 interface foundation and explicit progress/limitations on research and outside usefulness. A true claim of **sustained institutional growth** additionally requires repeated cross-task improvement cycles and longitudinal evidence; one two-task transfer is the first falsifiable test, not conclusive proof.

A live Gate alone passes M7, not the Alpha 1 strong success condition. A complete two-task test without a live Gate passes M2–M4, not M7. Neither is allowed to impersonate the other.

## 7. Founder-facing final handoff

The final report must include: actual verified Gate URL/decision/deadline, Task A artifact and receipt, M0/M1 code and independent comparison, Task B artifact and transfer result, routine Founder-intervention burden delta, continuity restore proof, RPG/Witness interface evidence, unresolved contradictions, deployment facts, and durable recovery entry point.

The terminal message may invite a real Founder decision only if the corresponding Gate is genuinely live; the **Alpha 1 institutional-success claim is conditional on the complete acceptance set**, not on the existence of the invitation. Confirmed blockers remain evidence-backed diagnoses, never a substitute finish line.
