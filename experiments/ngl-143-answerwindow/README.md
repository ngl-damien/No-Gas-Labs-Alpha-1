## Problem
An approved security-questionnaire answer can outlive its cited observation. A source may expire, be superseded, or contradict the answer before release. An answer assertion alone is not evidence of current control operation.

## Mechanism (locally built; commercial executable not published here)
ANSWERWINDOW evaluates structured, operator-supplied evidence at two dates: answer preparation and proposed release. It distinguishes HOLD, REVIEW, and BOUNDED, searches for unlinked contrary evidence, and generates SHA-256 integrity receipts. It does not authenticate source facts, test security controls, or certify compliance.
